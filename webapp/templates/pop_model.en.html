POPs site screening: where to look for strong contamination before laboratory analysis

The model estimates how likely a site is to be strongly contaminated with organochlorine pesticides (DDT+DDD+DDE or total HCH isomers ≥ 100 mg/kg, 1000 times the 0.1 mg/kg standard) from information available before laboratory analysis: the chemicals.tj survey form, location, contamination of neighbouring sampled sites, and open data (soil pH, terrain, population, flow accumulation, climate).

Limitations

  • The model sets the order of surveys but does not replace analysis: of the 20 sites with the highest score, about {{ 20 - summary.found_top20 }} will turn out not to be strongly contaminated.
  • Performance is moderate (AUC about 0.7). Seven feature sets were tried, so the best score is slightly optimistic; permutation test p ≈ 0.003.
  • The data come from five laboratories and sampling logs of 2012–2020 with different methods; samples were taken where contamination was expected.
  • Check of v1 on 8 sites in Panj and Kumsangir districts: all were moderately contaminated (3–44 mg/kg), while the model placed 6 of 8 in the “high” class; the score is overestimated at moderately contaminated sites.
  • Card text did not improve performance: text features are shown in the site cards as information but are not used in the model.
